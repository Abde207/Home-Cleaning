# 2. BUSINESS CONTEXT

Home Clean is a cleaning-service marketplace and dispatch platform initially targeting Jordan, beginning with Amman.

The fundamental business model is:

Customer
→ Home Clean
→ Dispatch
→ Partner Cleaning Company
→ Cleaning Team
→ Customer

The customer books through Home Clean.

The customer does NOT directly book with or manage the cleaning company.

Home Clean owns the customer relationship.

Partner cleaning companies provide the operational workforce behind the platform.

The platform must support:

* Customer mobile application
* Provider mobile application
* Home Clean Admin web dashboard
* Backend API
* Database
* Dispatch system
* Payments
* Cash collection
* Settlements
* Notifications
* Location/maps
* Ratings
* Support
* Promotions
* Audit logging
* Authentication
* Authorization

# 4. NON-NEGOTIABLE BUSINESS RULES

These rules must always remain true.

## 4.1 Backend owns business state

The backend is authoritative for:

* Booking status
* Assignment status
* Payment status
* Refund status
* Settlement status
* User authorization
* Pricing
* Dispatch decisions
* Financial calculations

Flutter and Admin applications must not independently determine authoritative business state.

---

## 4.2 Customer relationship

The customer interacts with Home Clean.

The customer does not directly manage the partner cleaning company.

Do not expose unnecessary internal provider information.

---

## 4.3 Provider isolation

A provider user must only access resources within their authorized company/team scope.

Never trust company IDs, team IDs, or role claims supplied by the client without server-side authorization.

---

## 4.4 Assignment is separate from Booking

Do NOT collapse assignment history into the booking.

A booking can have:

* multiple assignment attempts
* rejection
* reassignment
* manual reassignment
* dispatch retries

Historical assignments must remain auditable.

---

## 4.5 Historical data must remain historical

When important booking information is confirmed, preserve snapshots where specified.

Examples:

* booking address
* location
* service price
* extras
* pricing
* payment information

Changing the customer's current profile must not rewrite historical booking records.

---

## 4.6 Payments

Online payments must be server verified.

Never trust:

* client payment success
* client transaction status
* client amount
* client webhook-like data

Payment gateway webhooks must be verified.

---

## 4.7 Cash

Cash is an explicit payment flow.

Cash must not be treated as "online payment failed."

Cash collection and reconciliation must be auditable.

---

## 4.8 Financial separation

Keep separate concepts for:

* customer payment
* payment transaction
* platform commission
* provider payable
* settlement
* settlement payment

Do not merge them into one financial record.

---

## 4.9 Idempotency

Important operations must be safe against retries.

At minimum consider:

* booking creation
* payment initiation
* payment webhook processing
* assignment acceptance
* assignment rejection
* cash collection
* refund creation
* settlement operations

---

## 4.10 Auditability

Important administrative and financial actions must be logged.

Manual actions must identify:

* actor
* action
* affected resource
* timestamp
* relevant before/after information where appropriate
